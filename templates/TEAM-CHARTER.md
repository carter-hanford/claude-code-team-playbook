## How the seats work together

1. **Files are the protocol.** A seat reads a file before it asks a question.
2. **Messages carry three things:** handoffs, the lead's verdicts, and action-needed items.
3. **Correct only what changes a decision.** One round, never three.
4. **Check whether the lead is already in the loop** before relaying anything.
5. **One standup file.** Each seat appends its own entry when it finishes a working block.

## How the charter changes

- **Only the lead widens or narrows a lane.** A seat earns a wider lane by doing its own well
  and offering value past its edge. It never asks for one.
- **Seats that hand work back and forth more than they do it get merged.**
